---
name: Audit CAR due-date policy
description: Business deadlines for defined finding classifications and historical CAR number recovery.
---

CAR due dates are calendar dates calculated from the CAR's original Created Date: Major NC +2 days, Minor NC +3 days, OFI +4 days. Do not silently treat Moderate NC as Minor NC, or invent a deadline for Observation or another unlisted classification. Preserve an existing date for unlisted historical types unless the business owner supplies a new rule.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined only three classification deadlines; production contains Moderate NC and Observation too, but their deadlines were not specified.

**How to apply:** Show the calculated date consistently in the CAR register and Audit PowerPoint, including older CARs. An approved extension overrides the original date in the report. Ask for an explicit business rule before giving other classifications an SLA.
