---
name: Audit CAR due-date policy
description: Business deadlines for defined finding classifications and historical CAR number recovery.
---

CAR due dates are calendar dates calculated from the explicitly recorded Finding Date, not CAR creation: Major NC +2 days, Minor NC +3 days, OFI +4 days. Do not silently treat Moderate NC as Minor NC, or invent a deadline for Observation or another unlisted classification. Preserve an existing date for unlisted historical types unless the business owner supplies a new rule.

**Why:** The user changed the deadline base from CAR creation to Finding Date. They explicitly defined only three classification deadlines; production contains Moderate NC and Observation too, but their deadlines were not specified.

**How to apply:** New findings require a date in the entry form. Authorized users may set or correct the date on existing findings while the Audit Report is editable, recalculating linked CAR deadlines. Show the calculated date consistently in the CAR register and downloads. An approved extension overrides the original date in the report. Keep historical saved deadlines until a real Finding Date is supplied; do not fabricate dates from CAR creation. Ask for an explicit business rule before giving other classifications an SLA.
