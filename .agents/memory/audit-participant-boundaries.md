---
name: Audit participant boundaries
description: Scope and historical-display rules for selecting operational Audit participants.
---

Individual-audit participants must have an active Audit assignment for that project's scope or a true organization-wide assignment. Empty projects on a department-only assignment do not mean organization-wide. A scoped lead marker must come from a qualifying assignment, not another project's marker combined with generic local membership.

**Why:** The requested participant restriction is operational selection eligibility, not a change to application access, permissions or role configuration. Broad options previously offered other projects' users.

**How to apply:** Derive Plan scope from its actual individual schedule. Preserve legacy project-role participation in Process audits and explicit department matching. Keep saved names separate from eligible choices; retained invalid IDs need explicit correction, never silent removal. Unavailable eligibility must be explicit; broad offline user snapshots cannot authorize participants.
