---
name: Audit Plan selection scope
description: Product scope boundaries for method and criteria selections in Audit Plans
---

Audit Method and Audit Criteria are Plan-only selections from fixed supplied lists. Do not extend them to report templates or add Sampling Basis or company-procedure free-text fields unless separately requested.

**Why:** The user explicitly excluded changes to downloaded/emailed templates, configurable master lists, and those extra questions when requesting the selections.

**How to apply:** Treat future export or master-data support as a separate product decision, not an implied consequence of extending the Audit Plan form.
