---
name: CAR approval policy
description: Explicit CAR approval scope, legacy compatibility and shared-level product boundaries.
---

CAR approvals are an independent process selected in the Audit role editor. A role uses its existing shared Approval Level independently in each selected process; do not introduce process-specific level fields or infer CAR approval from Team Lead authorization.

**Why:** The user requested configurable CAR approval levels without changing Schedule/Report routing or automatically granting existing Team Lead roles new powers.

**How to apply:** New submissions and resubmissions require explicit CAR approvers. Preserve in-flight route order when role levels change, keep same-level reviewers as alternatives, and retain live scope/assignment eligibility checks.

Only legacy CARs already pending without a saved route keep the original assigned-Plan-Lead review. New routed CARs close only on final acceptance. Keep extension review separate, without allowing it or ordinary administration to bypass unfinished response approval.

**Why:** The user explicitly required legacy completion and unchanged closed history, not retroactive rerouting or reopening.

**How to apply:** Query/rework unlocks owner corrections; resubmission restarts configured levels while retaining decision history. Do not alter numbering, response ownership or Word templates when extending this workflow.
