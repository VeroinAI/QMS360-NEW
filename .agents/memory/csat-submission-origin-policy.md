---
name: CSAT submission origin
description: Business distinction between customer-submitted surveys and employee submissions on a customer's behalf.
---

Customers may submit their own satisfaction surveys, or internal employees may submit on customers' behalf. Internal employee submissions go through review/approval and may be sent back for corrections. Customer-submitted surveys must not be sent back.

**Why:** The user explicitly distinguished these two business paths when asking for implementation recommendations.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction when designing CSAT submission and review. The detailed statuses, customer verification method, review actions and treatment of existing surveys still require agreement; recommendations made during discussion are not approved implementation decisions.
