---
name: CSAT Word report policy
description: Shared Word-based CSAT downloads and truthful mapping of saved survey responses.
---

The CSAT landing-row Download and individual report Download must use the same customer-supplied Word form as PDF, retaining its Algihaz branding, purple headings, controlled footer and checkbox layout.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the same report at both entry points, not independent formats or changes to other QA/QC exports.

**How to apply:** Use actual saved survey ratings and qualitative responses. Never treat the report submitter as the customer/respondent. Optional customer/contact/follow-up details not collected by the entry form are unmapped, not invented. The template's recommendation option “Maybe” must not silently replace the application's actual response “Partially”; print the recorded answer. Preserve long feedback on clearly labelled continuation pages rather than clipping it or reducing it to unreadable text.
