---
name: Daily Submission Word report policy
description: Customer Word layout, saved-baseline semantics and shared Daily PDF downloads.
---

Daily Submission landing-row Download and individual report Download must produce the same PDF based on the supplied daily document-governance Word layout. Preserve its section order, colours, tables and change indicators. Unmatched fields must read exactly “To be mapped”.

**Why:** The user explicitly required the Word design and identical downloads at both entry points. The attachment contains illustrative identities, numbers and a reviewer-only appendix, none of which belong in issued reports.

**How to apply:** Populate actual saved report data and the report's saved previous-submission baseline, not a newly queried baseline that could change historical results. The form's absent previous counts start at zero; absent current input is not permission to copy sample numbers. Match revisions by document category and normalized revision name. Use configured project-team names and actual preparation/submission identity, never a presumed Document Controller title or signature. Remove sample-data headers and reviewer instructions from issued output. Retain explicit zero values, conditional first-report notices and all repeated revision rows. Calibri is not bundled in the supplied file; embed the compatible Carlito fonts and disclose the substitution.

The separate Daily Dashboard PDF follows the customer's four-page presentation and accompanying mapping workbook. It supplements, rather than replaces, the Word-layout daily report. Both exports must retain the saved-baseline semantics above.

**Why:** The user requested separate Report PDF and Dashboard PDF actions. The workbook's matching sample numbers are illustrative, not new validation rules.

**How to apply:** Do not force document-type counts to equal discipline totals, or discipline Under Review counts to equal the pending-review workspace. The dashboard's Overall Under Review counts only the Under Review status across parties, while pending totals and ageing include every review status. Do not silently clamp negative corrections into positive doughnut segments.
