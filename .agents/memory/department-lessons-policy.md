---
name: Department Lessons access and numbering
description: User-approved scope and numbering rules for Department Lessons.
---

Lessons may belong to either a Project or a Department. Departments come from the existing Department Master, not artificial projects or a parallel master.

Department Lessons require explicit department-scoped role assignments. A project-only assignment must not grant department access, and a department-only assignment must never be interpreted as organization-wide merely because it has no projects.

Drona's external project-membership restriction must remain intact for project Lessons without erasing QMS-controlled department permissions. Keep those two scopes separate, including for administration and evidence access.

Reference numbers use an independent sequence per department. Preserve existing project numbering and all previously issued references.

The official Lessons PDF includes a Project / Department row followed by the selected source's label and name, while retaining the supplied branding and form content.

**Why:** On 2026-10-21 the user explicitly chose department-specific role assignments and separate department sequences when adding Department Lessons.

**How to apply:** Carry source identity and capability-coupled scope through future Lessons forms, lists, approvals, evidence, notifications, offline changes and exports. Never substitute a dummy project for a department.
