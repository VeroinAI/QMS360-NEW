---
name: Lessons approval history
description: Business requirements for the Lesson Learned Approval record and legacy history gaps.
---

The Lesson Learned Approval record must show every recorded approval cycle, newest action first, including submissions, send-backs, approvals and reassignment details. Do not substitute only the latest form fields or the activity timeline's latest-page subset.

**Why:** The user needs to inspect the entire approval history, including earlier correction comments, rather than losing visibility whenever a lesson is resubmitted.

**How to apply:** Preserve each recorded action and its available actor, time and comments. Current form metadata may fill missing legacy events, but must not duplicate recorded actions or invent earlier cycles. Do not present a current profile signature as evidence of an unrelated historical approval.
