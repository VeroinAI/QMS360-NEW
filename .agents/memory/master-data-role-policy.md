---
name: Master Data role assignments
description: User-confirmed informational semantics of single or multiple roles assigned to Master Data values.
---

Master Data role assignments are informational only. They must not restrict dropdown visibility or selection, grant permissions, or automatically choose assignees.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose “Save role assignments only” rather than role-based visibility and selection restrictions.

**How to apply:** Preserve assigned roles when values are saved and edited, alongside other value configuration. Do not turn these references into access rules or workflow defaults without a new explicit requirement.
