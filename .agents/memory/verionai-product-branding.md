---
name: VerionAI product branding
description: The naming boundary and visual direction for AI assistance throughout QMS360.
---

All user-facing AI assistants, actions, results, and status messages in QMS360
must use the VerionAI name. Keep infrastructure terminology such as AI provider,
model, connector keys, API contracts, and backend identifiers unchanged.

**Why:** VerionAI is the product identity for the branded agents. Generic AI
labels make the embedded assistants look unrelated, while renaming technical
provider concepts would make administration less clear.

**How to apply:** Use the shared VerionAI identity for new assistant surfaces:
magenta wordmark and sparkle mark, a pale-pink supporting tint, dark companion
text, accessible dark-mode colors, and explicit human-review language where
appropriate.
